Candle Care

Make your glow last longer

A few small rituals for a cleaner, longer, more beautiful burn.

Trim the wick

Keep the wick trimmed to about ¼ inch (6 mm) before every light to avoid smoke and soot.

First burn matters

Let the top layer melt evenly across before blowing out, so your candle doesn't tunnel.

Up to 3–4 hours

Don't burn for more than 3–4 hours at a time. Let it cool fully before relighting.

Burn safely

Never leave a lit candle unattended. Keep away from drafts, curtains, children and pets.

Questions

Good to know

How do I place an order?

Tap "Order on WhatsApp" on any candle. Your order details are filled in automatically, so just add your name and address and hit send. We'll confirm availability, delivery charges and payment on chat.

Do you deliver across India?

Yes, we ship pan-India. Delivery time and charges depend on your pincode, and we'll share them when you message us.

How do I pay?

Once your order is confirmed on WhatsApp, we'll share the payment options with you.

Can I get custom colours or bulk orders?

Absolutely. We love making wedding favours, return gifts and corporate hampers. Message us with the occasion, quantity and date, and we'll craft something just for you.

Is gift wrapping available?

Every Aurévo candle is packed to be gift-ready. Ask us about personalised tags and message cards.

How should I store my candle?

Keep it in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight to keep the colour and shape at their best.
